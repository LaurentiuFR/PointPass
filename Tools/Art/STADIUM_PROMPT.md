Produce one landscape rectangular image, approximately 5:3 aspect ratio, filled edge to edge with realistic closely mown healthy stadium turf. Camera strictly vertical overhead, no perspective. Rich natural medium emerald green with restrained alternating vertical mowing bands from left to right; subtle fine grass blade grain, small organic tonal variation and brushed mowing texture. Pure grass only: NO white lines, NO pitch markings, NO center circle, NO goals, NO stands, NO players, NO people, NO objects, NO shadows, NO text, NO logos, NO border. Opaque full image, no transparency. The engine draws accurate white markings separately on top. Seamless-looking edge continuity is desirable. Premium sports-game turf, realistic material but restrained detail, not muddy, not noisy, not neon.
